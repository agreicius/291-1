Theorem 1.18.20. Fundamental spaces and row operations.

Let \(A\in \ML_{m,n}(F)\text{,}\) and suppose \(A\) is row equivalent to \(B\text{.}\)
  1. \(\NS A=\NS B\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\RS A=\RS B\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. In general \(\CS A \) is not equal to \(\CS B\text{.}\) However, letting \(\bolda_i, \boldb_i\in F^m\) be the columns of \(A\) and \(B\text{,}\) respectively, the columns \(\bolda_{i_1}, \bolda_{i_2},\dots, \bolda_{i_r}\) form a basis of \(\CS A\) if and only if the columns \(\boldb_{i_1}, \boldb_{i_2},\dots, \boldb_{i_r}\) form a basis of \(\CS B\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
🔗
