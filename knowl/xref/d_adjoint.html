Definition 1.14.12. Adjoint matrix.

Let \(A\in \ML_{n,n}(F)\text{,}\) and let \(I=\{1,2\dots, n\}\times \{1,2,\dots, n\}\text{.}\) The adjoint matrix of \(A\text{,}\) denoted \(\adj A\text{,}\) is the \(n\times n\) matrix defined as
\begin{equation*} \adj A=[(-1)^{i+j}M_{j,i}]_{(i,j)\in I}=\left( [(-1)^{i+j}M_{i,j}]_{(i,j)\in I}\right)^T\text{.} \end{equation*}
