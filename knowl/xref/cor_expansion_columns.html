Corollary 1.13.10. Expansion along columns.

Let \(A=[a_{i,j}]_{n\times n}\in \ML_{n,n}(F)\text{.}\) For any \(1\leq j\leq n\) we have
\begin{equation*} \det A= \sum_{k=1}^n(-1)^{k+j}a_{k,j}M_{k,j}\text{.} \end{equation*}
In other words, we can compute \(\det A\) by expanding along any column of \(A\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.

For any \(1\leq j\leq n\text{,}\) we have
\begin{align*} \sum_{k=1}^n(-1)^{k+j}a_{k,j}M_{k,j}\amp =\sum_{k=1}^n(-1)^{k+j}(A^T)_{j,k}\det A_{j,k} \amp (\knowl{./knowl/xref/d_transpose.html}{\text{Definition 1.9.18}})\\ \amp = \sum_{k=1}^n(-1)^{k+j}(A^T)_{j,k}\det (A^T)_{j,k} \amp ( (A^T)_{i,j}= A_{j,i}) \\ \amp= \det A^T \amp (\knowl{./knowl/xref/d_det.html}{\text{Definition 1.13.3}})\\ \amp=\det A \amp (\knowl{./knowl/xref/th_det_transpose.html}{\text{Theorem 1.13.9}}) \text{.} \end{align*}
