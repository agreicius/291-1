Definition 1.8.1. Matrices.

Let \(F\) be a field, let \(m\) and \(n\) be positive integers, and let
\begin{equation*} I = \{1,2,\dots, m\}\times \{1,2,\dots, n\}=\{(i,j)\mid 1\leq i\leq m, 1\leq j\leq n\} \text{.} \end{equation*}
An \(F\)-valued matrix of size (or dimension) \(m\times n\) is an \(F\)-valued tuple \((a_{(i,j)})_{(i,j)\in I}\) indexed by \(I\text{.}\) We will write \(a_{i,j}\) instead of \(a_{(i,j)}\text{,}\) and call \(a_{i,j}\) the \(i,j\)-th entry of the matrix. Additionally, to distinguish matrices from \(n\)-tuples, one the following bracket notations will be used:
\begin{align*} [a_{i,j}]_{(i,j)\in I} \amp \amp [a_{i,j}]_{\substack{1\leq i\leq m\\ 1\leq j\leq n}} \amp \amp [a_{i,j}]_{m\times n} \text{.} \end{align*}
We will favor capital letters (i.e., \(A, B, C,\dots\)) when naming matrices, and will often display a matrix \(A=[a_{i,j}]_{m\times n}\) as a rectangular array as
\begin{equation*} A=\genmatrix\text{,} \end{equation*}
where the entry in the \(i\)-th row and \(j\)-th column of the array is \(a_{i,j}\text{.}\) The set \(F^I\) of all \(F\)-valued \(m\times n\) matrices is denoted \(\ML_{m,n}(F)\text{:}\) i.e.,
\begin{equation} \ML_{m,n}(F)=\{[a_{i,j}]_{m\times n}\mid a_{i,j}\in F \text{ for all } 1\leq i\leq m, 1\leq j\leq n\}\text{.}\tag{1.8.1} \end{equation}
