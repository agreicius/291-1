Definition 1.15.10. Null space of matrix.

Let \(A\in \ML_{m,n}(F)\text{.}\) The null space of \(A\text{,}\) denoted \(\NS A\text{,}\) is the set of all solutions to the matrix equation
\begin{equation} A\boldx=\underset{m\times 1}{\boldzero}\text{.}\tag{1.15.1} \end{equation}
In other words,
\begin{equation*} \NS A=\{\boldx\in F^n\colon A\boldx=\boldzero\}\text{.} \end{equation*}
Equivalently, thinking in terms of linear systems, \(\NS A\) is the set of solutions to the homogeneous linear system represented by (1.15.1).
