Procedure 1.22.10 Contracting and extending to bases in general spaces.

Let \(V\) be an \(F\)-vector space of dimension \(n\text{,}\) and let \(S=(\boldv_1, \boldv_2,\dots, \boldv_r)\) be a tuple of vectors of \(V\text{.}\)
  • Contracting to a basis.
    Let \(W=\Span S\text{.}\) To contract \(S\) to a basis \(W\text{,}\) proceed as follows.
    1. Pick any ordered basis \(B\) of \(V\) and let \(\tilde{S}=([\boldv_1]_B, [\boldv_2]_B, \dots, [\boldv_r]_B)\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Use the relevant procedure of Procedure 1.18.31 to find a subset \(J\subseteq \{1,2,\dots, r\}\) such that \(([\boldv_{j}]_B)_{j\in J}\) is a basis for \(\Span \tilde{S}\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    3. The tuple \((\boldv_j)_{j\in J}\text{,}\) a subtuple of \(S\text{,}\) is a basis for \(W=\Span S\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  • Extending to a basis.
    Assume \(S\) is linearly independent. To extend \(S\) to a basis of \(V\) proceed as follows.
    1. Pick any ordered basis \(B\) of \(V\) and let \(\tilde{S}=([\boldv_1]_B, [\boldv_2]_B, \dots, [\boldv_r]_B)\subseteq F^n\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Use the relevant procedure of Procedure 1.18.31 to extend \(\tilde{S}\) to a basis \(([\boldv_1]_B, [\boldv_2]_B, \dots, [\boldv_r]_B, \boldx_{r+1}, \dots, \boldx_n)\) of \(F^n\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    3. By (3) of Theorem 1.22.2, for all \(r+1\leq i\leq n\) there are vectors \(\boldv_{i}\in V\) satisfying \([\boldv_i]_B=\boldx_i\text{.}\) The tuple \((\boldv_1,\dots, \boldv_r,\boldv_{r+1},\dots,\boldv_n)\) is a basis of \(V\) containing \(S\) as a subtuple.
      🔗
      🔗
