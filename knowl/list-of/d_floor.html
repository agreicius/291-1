Definition 1.2.4. Floor function.

Given \(r\in \R\text{,}\) we denote by \(\floor{r}\) the unique integer \(n\in \Z\) satisfying \(n\leq r< n+1\text{.}\) The function
\begin{align*} \floor{\phantom{x}}\colon \R \amp \rightarrow \R\\ r \amp \mapsto \floor{r} \end{align*}
is called the floor function.
