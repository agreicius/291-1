Theorem 1.16.13. Division algorithm for polynomials.

Let \(F\) be a field, let \(f,g\in F[x]\text{,}\) and suppose \(g\ne \boldzero\text{.}\) There is a unique pair of polynomials \((q,r)\in F[x]\times F[x]\) satisfying the following conditions.
  1. \(f=qg+r\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(r=\boldzero\) or \(0\leq \deg r< \deg g\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
🔗
