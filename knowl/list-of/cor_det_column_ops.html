Corollary 1.14.9. Determinant and column operations.

Let \(A\in \ML_{n,n}(F)\text{.}\)
  1. For all \(1\leq i\leq n\) and \(c\in F\text{,}\) we have
    \begin{equation*} \det(A\, \underset{c\cdot r_i}{E})=c\det(A)\text{.} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. For all \(1\leq i< j\leq n\text{,}\) we have
    \begin{equation*} \det(A\, \underset{r_i\leftrightarrow r_j}{E})=-\det(A) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. For all \(1\leq i,j\leq n\) and \(c\in F\text{,}\) we have
    \begin{equation*} \det(A\, \underset{r_i+cr_j}{E})=\det(A)\text{.} \end{equation*}

    🔗
