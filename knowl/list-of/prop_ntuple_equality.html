Proposition 1.5.2. \(n\)-tuple equality.

Let \(F\) be a field, and let \(n\) be a positive integer. Given \(v=(a_i)_{i=1}^n, w=(b_i)_{i=1}^n\in F^n\text{,}\) we have
\begin{equation} v=w \text{ if and only if } a_i=b_i \text{ for all } i\in \{1,2,\dots, n\}\text{.}\tag{1.5.1} \end{equation}
