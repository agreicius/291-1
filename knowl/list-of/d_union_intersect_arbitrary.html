Definition 0.1.18. Arbitrary unions and intersections.

Let \(A\) be a set, and let \((A_i)_{i\in I}\) be a family of sets with \(A_i\subseteq A\) for all \(i\in I\text{.}\)
  • The union of the \(A_i\text{,}\) denoted \(\bigcup_{i\in I}A_i\text{,}\) is defined as
    \begin{align} \bigcup_{i\in I}A_i \amp =\{a\in A\mid a\in A_i\text{ for some } i\in I\}\text{.}\tag{0.1.2} \end{align}
    🔗
    🔗
  • The intersection of the \(A_i\text{,}\) denoted \(\bigcap_{i\in I}A_i\text{,}\) is defined as
    \begin{align} \bigcap_{i\in I}A_i \amp =\{a\in A\mid a\in A_i\text{ for all } i\in I\}\text{.}\tag{0.1.3} \end{align}
