Definition 1.2.9. Subtraction, division, integer powers.

Let \(F\) be a field.
  1. Subtraction.
    Given elements \(a, b\text{,}\) we define their difference \(a-b\) as
    \begin{equation*} a-b=a+\, -b\text{.} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Division.
    Given elements \(a,b\in F\) with \(b\ne 0\text{,}\) we define their quotient \(a/b\) as
    \begin{equation*} a/b=a\, b^{-1}\text{.} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Integer powers.
    Given any \(a\in F\text{,}\) we define \(a^0=1\) and
    \begin{equation*} a^{n}=\underset{n \text{ times}}{\underbrace{a\cdot a \cdots a}} \end{equation*}
    where \(n\) is any positive integer.
    🔗
    Furthermore, if \(a\ne 0\) and \(n\geq 0\text{,}\) we define
    \begin{equation*} a^{-n}=(a^{-1})^n\text{.} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
