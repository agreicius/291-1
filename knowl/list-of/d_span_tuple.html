Definition 1.6.11. Span of tuple of vectors.

Let \((v_i)_{i\in I}\) be a tuple of vectors in the \(F\)-vector space \(V\text{.}\) We define the span of \((v_i)_{i\in I}\text{,}\) denoted \(\Span (v_i)_{i\in I}\text{,}\) to be the span of the set \(\{v_i\mid i\in I}\text{:}\)i.e.,
\begin{equation*} \Span(v_i)_{i\in I}=\Span \{v_i\mid i\in I}\text{.} \end{equation*}
Note that in the case of the empty tuple, we have \(\Span()=\Span\{\}=\boldzero\text{.}\)
