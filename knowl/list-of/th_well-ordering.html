Theorem 1.1.6. Well-ordering princple.

Let \(b\) be an integer. Given any nonempty subset \(A\) of \(\Z_{\geq b}\text{,}\) \(A\) has a least element: i.e., there is an element \(a\in A\) such that \(a\leq a'\) for all \(a'\in A\text{.}\)
