Theorem 1.5.3. \(F^n\) vector space structure.

Let \(F\) be a field, and let \(n\) be a positive integer. The set \(F^n\) of all \(F\)-valued \(n\)-tuples is a vector space with respect to the operations defined as
\begin{align*} (a_i)_{i=1}^n+(b_i)_{i=1}^n\amp =(a_i)_{i=1}^n+(b_i)_{i=1}^n \\ c(a_i)_{i=1}^n\amp =(ca_i)_{i=1}^n \end{align*}
for all \((a_i)_{i=1}^n, (b_i)_{i=1}^n\in F^n\) and \(c\in F\text{.}\)
