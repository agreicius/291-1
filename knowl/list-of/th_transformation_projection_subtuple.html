Theorem 1.19.20. Projection onto subtuple.

Let \(F\) be a field, \(I\) a nonempty set, and \(J\) a nonempty subset of \(I\text{.}\) The function \(T\colon F^I\rightarrow F^J\) defined as \(T\left((a_i)_{i\in I}\right)=(a_j)_{j\in J}\) is a linear transformation.
