Proposition 1.11.18. Elementary matrices and row operations.

Let \(A\in \ML_{m,n}(F)\text{.}\)
  1. Let \(1\leq i\leq m\text{,}\) and let \(c\in F\) be a nonzero scalar. The matrix \(\underset{cr_i}{E}A\) is the result of multiplying the \(i\)-th row of \(A\) by \(c\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Let \(i,j\in \{1,2,\dots, m\}\) be distinct indices. The matrix \(\underset{r_i\leftrightarrow r_j}{E}A\) is the result of swapping the \(i\)-th and \(j\)-th rows of \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Let \(1\leq i,j\leq m\text{,}\) and let \(c\in F\text{.}\) The matrix \(\underset{r_i+c\,r_j}{E}A\) is the result of adding \(c\) times the \(j\)-th row of \(A\) to the \(i\)-th row of \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
🔗

Proof.

The proof is left as an exercise.
