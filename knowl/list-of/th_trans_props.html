Theorem 1.10.13. Properties of matrix transposition.

In what follows \(m,n,r\) will denote positive integers.
  1. \((A+B)^T=A^T+B^T\) for all \(A,B\in \ML_{m,n}(F)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \((cA)^T=cA^T\) for all \(c\in F\) and \(A\in \ML_{m,n}(F)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \((AB)^T=B^TA^T\) for all \(A\in \ML_{m,r}(F), B\in \ML_{r,n}(F)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\left(A^T\right)^T=A\) for all \(A\in \ML_{m,n}(F)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
🔗

Proof.

We prove only the first statement. First observe that if \(A\) is \(m\times n\text{,}\) then so is \(B\) and \(A+B\text{.}\) Then \((A+B)^T\) is \(n\times m\) by Definition 1.9.18. Similarly, we see that \(A^T+B^T\) is \(n\times m\text{.}\)
🔗
Next, given any \((i,j)\) with \(1\leq i\leq n\text{,}\) \(1\leq j\leq m\text{,}\) we have
\begin{align*} \left((A+B)^T\right)_{ij}\amp =(A+B)_{ji} \amp (\knowl{./knowl/xref/d_transpose.html}{\text{Definition 1.9.18}})\\ \amp = A_{ji}+B_{ji} \amp \\ \amp =(A^T)_{ij}+(B^T)_{ij} \amp (\knowl{./knowl/xref/d_transpose.html}{\text{Definition 1.9.18}} \\ \amp =(A^T+B^T)_{ij} \amp \text{.} \end{align*}
Since the \(ij\)-entries of both matrices are equal for each \((i,j)\text{,}\) it follows that \((A+B)^T=A^T+B^T\text{.}\)
