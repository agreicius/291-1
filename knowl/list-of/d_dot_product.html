Definition 1.9.12. Dot product.

Given \(n\)-vectors \(\boldv=(a_1,a_2,\dots, a_n)\) and \(\boldw=(b_1,b_2,\dots, b_n)\text{,}\) their dot product, denoted \(\boldv\cdot\boldw\text{,}\) is defined as
\begin{equation} \boldv\cdot \boldw=\sum_{i=1}^na_ib_i=a_1b_1+a_2b_2+\cdots +a_nb_n\text{.}\tag{1.9.3} \end{equation}
The operation
\begin{align*} \R^n\times \R^n \amp \rightarrow \R\\ (\boldv,\boldw) \amp \longmapsto \boldv\cdot\boldw \end{align*}
is called the dot product on \(\R^n\text{.}\)
