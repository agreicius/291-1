Theorem 1.14.10. Determinant: unique characterization.

Let \(V=F^n\) where \(n\) is a positive integer, and treat \(\det\) as a map on \(V^n\text{.}\)
  1. \(\det\) is the unique alternating multilinear map on \(V^n\) that further satisfies
    \begin{equation*} \det(\bolde_1,\bolde_2,\dots, \bolde_n)=\det I_n=1\text{.} \end{equation*}

    \begin{equation*} f(\bolde_1,\bolde_2,\dots, \bolde_n)=f(I_n)=c\text{.} \end{equation*}
