Example 1.18.28. Using the rank-nullity theorem.

Suppose \(A\) is a \(3\times 7\) matrix, and that \(\nullity A=4\text{.}\) Show that \(\CS A=\R^3\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
By the rank-nullity theorem we have
\begin{align*} \dim\CS A \amp =7-\dim \NS A \\ \amp =7-4\\ \amp = 3\text{.} \end{align*}
Since \(\CS A\subseteq \R^3\) and \(\dim \CS A=\dim \R^3\text{,}\) we conclude by Corollary 1.18.10 that \(\CS A=\R^3\text{,}\) as desired.
