Definition 1.5.4. Special elements of \(F^n\).

Let \(F\) be a field, and let \(n\) be a positive integer.
  • Zero vector.
    The zero vector of \(F^n\text{,}\) denoted \(\boldzero_n\) (or \(\boldzero\) for short), is the \(n\)-tuple, all of whose entries are equal to \(0\text{:}\) i.e., \(\boldzero_n=(0)_{i=1}^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • Vector inverse.
    Given an \(n\)-tuple \(v=(a_i)_{i=1}^n\in F^n\text{,}\) the vector inverse of \(v\text{,}\) denoted \(-v\text{,}\) is the element of \(F^n\) defined as
    \begin{equation*} -v=(-a_i)_{i=1}^n\text{.} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  • Indicator vectors.
    For each \(i\in \{1,2,\dots, n\}\text{,}\) the \(i\)-th standard basis element of \(F^n\text{,}\) denoted \(\bolde_i\text{,}\) is the \(n\)-tuple whose \(i\)-th entry is equal to \(1\) and whose \(j\)-th entry is equal to \(0\) for all \(j\ne i\text{.}\)
