Definition 1.13.3. The determinant.

Let \(A=[a_{ij}]_{n\times n}\in \ML_{n,n}(F)\text{.}\) The determinant is defined as follows:
  • Base case: \(n=1\).
    When \(n=1\) we have \(A=[a_{1,1}]\) and we define \(\det A=a_{1,1}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • Recursive case: \(n\geq 2 \).
    When \(n\geq 2\) we define
    \begin{align} \det A \amp = \sum_{j=1}^n(-1)^{1+j}a_{1,j}\det A_{1, j} \tag{1.13.1}\\ \amp = a_{11}\det A_{1,1}-a_{1,2} A_{1,2}+\cdots +(-1)^{1+n}a_{1,n}\det A_{1, n}\text{.}\tag{1.13.2} \end{align}
